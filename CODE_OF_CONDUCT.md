# Code of Conduct

InsumerAPI's repositories follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep discussion on the work, and do not harass anyone. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do.

Report unacceptable behavior to support@insumermodel.com. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept confidential.
